# Kdenlive for Android

Unofficial port of the [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org) video editor to Android (arm64). Desktop Kdenlive (Qt 6, KDE Frameworks 6, MLT 7), not a rewrite. Built for Pixel-class phones in landscape with a mouse and keyboard; touch and portrait are still basic.

**[Watch the demo (MP4)](demo/nature-montage.mp4)**: a title, three clips, credits and music edited on a Pixel 9 Pro, exported, and played back. Media credits: [demo/CREDITS.md](demo/CREDITS.md).

- Build: [docs/BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md)
- Patches for upstream: [docs/UPSTREAMING.md](docs/UPSTREAMING.md), [patches/](patches/)

Kdenlive, MLT, FFmpeg, x264, OpenCV, frei0r and the LADSPA plugins keep their own licences; the combined app is GPL.
